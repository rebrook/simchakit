3 · Contrast check

Measured on the design tokens. Text on surfaces, the red tint and the badges passes 4.5:1 in light and dark (secondary text 7.6:1, red text on the tint 7.0:1 light and 5.9:1 dark). Two things fail and are fixed in V5-O1b.

  • White text on a filled accent fails in dark mode (2.1:1) and on four light palettes: Navy & Gold 2.8, Blush 3.2, Rose 3.9, Teal 4.1. Dark ink text passes on Navy & Gold (6.3), Blush (5.5) and Rose (4.6). Teal fails with both, so its fill needs darkening. The mocks above pick the text colour per theme and the banner is a solid accent, because dark text on a dark-to-light gradient fails on its dark end (seen on Navy & Gold).
  • --text-muted fails as text (2.9 to 3.5:1). Use --text-secondary for labels, subtitles and meta lines; keep muted for disabled and decorative use.

What changes

  • List at full column width; one red tint, only on overdue rows; no left rule.
  • Titles name vendor, amount and due date; badges only when they add information.
  • Thank-yous open Gifts filtered to owed notes. Per-gift "Mark written" stays in Gifts.
  • Greeting skips a leading "The"; with no usable name it reads "Welcome back."
  • Timeline shows the next three entries, or the last three once the event is over, plus a link.
  • The new-event card stays and appears only after the event has completed, as one quiet row of about 64px at the bottom of the left column (last on phone).
  • Phone: 2×2 stats, one-line banner, list first.
