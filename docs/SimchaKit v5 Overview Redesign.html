Overview: hero concept, merged

Revised after the second round of team feedback. Hero: the concept's scale and spacing restored (name 68px, countdown 200px, monogram 112px, padding 48 and 56px), no schedule chips and no eyebrow, the motif confined to the right and faded so the name sits on clean colour, a distinct background per palette, text colours derived from each background, a lifted hero with an accent edge in dark mode, and logo and photo states (use the Image control). New: a Celebration schedule card under the hero, grouped by day, with times, venues, confirmed counts, a Family only row with an arrive-by time, and Copy schedule. Rehearsal and minyans sit in the same card behind one line, "Before the weekend", so the separate Prep dates card is gone. Previous versions: v3, v2.

Palette check, all nine

Computed live. Hero: each palette gets a deep background (Navy & Gold uses the brand navy); the accent is lightened until it reaches 4.5:1 on it. Page: the accent as text on paper, and the filled button with its text colour. In production these should be precomputed per palette in App.css, not computed at runtime.

Notes for the dev

  • Celebration schedule rows come from the timeline entries and link to each one's details. Past entries are not listed. Rows show start time, name, venue and confirmed count with the adult and kid split, grouped by day.
  • Two new optional fields on timeline entries: an audience flag (family only: hides the count and shows a tag) and an arrive-by time (shown after the venue). Without them the row shows the start time only. Guests never see either.
  • Copy schedule copies plain text for pasting into a message: day headings, time, name, building and room, plus each building's address once at the end. Family-only entries are left out. Await clipboard.writeText and show Could not copy on failure; announce the result through a live region that already exists in the page.
  • Earlier dates holds entries before the first day of the celebration and opens in place (aria-expanded). Hidden when there are none. After the event the whole schedule card is hidden and the thank-you work takes its place. On the day, past rows read Done and the next row is highlighted Up next.
  • Reading order: render the cards in the order they should be read and tabbed (Needs you now, schedule, Who's coming, Readiness, Quick Notes) and place them with grid areas on desktop. Do not reorder with CSS order.
  • Photo hero: a scrim sits between the photo and the countdown; check the countdown against the photo plus scrim with a busy real photo. Photo and logo uploads need a storage and plan-limit check.
  • Havdalah: when the main date is a Shabbat or festival and an evening event follows, the day hero shows "Shabbat ends 6:14 PM" under that event and the schedule row carries "Havdalah 6:14 PM". Times come from the Hebcal Shabbat Times API for the venue location; the sample time here is made up.
  • Last two weeks on phone: the hero's Next line shows the next event's time, so the next gathering is visible without scrolling past Needs you now.
  • Hebrew date and parashah come from Hebcal (CC-BY, attribution needed; the JS library is GPL, so use the REST API or leave it out).
  • Motif is chosen by event type (star for mitzvahs, lattice for weddings, none for the rest) and is a faded layer on the right half only. A host photo replaces it: duotoned in the palette, behind the countdown, masked to fade into the colour. A host logo replaces the monogram in the ring.
  • Hero colours are precomputed per palette in App.css (background, accent, text), light and dark, with the contrast table above as the test.
  • Runway milestones use dates the app holds. Start at the earliest milestone or 90 days out, show at most five, merge the rest into "+N more".
